GOOD QUESTIONS

Frequently Asked Questions

Here's the stuff most business owners want to know before getting started.

How long does it take to build my website?

Most straightforward small-business websites can move from kickoff to launch quickly once we have the information, photos and approvals we need. We'll give you a realistic timeline before work begins.

Can I use my existing domain name?

Absolutely. If you already own a domain, we can connect it to your new site. If you need one, we'll help you choose and register it in your name.

What happens when I need a change?

Send us the update. Routine website maintenance is built into our plans; larger additions or new functionality can be quoted separately before we do the work.

Will my website work on phones?

Yes. Every DawgsWeb site is built to be responsive so it works across phones, tablets and desktop computers.

Do you help with Google and SEO?

Yes. Every site gets solid SEO foundations, and our higher plans include expanded local SEO and Google measurement/search tools.

Are there unlimited changes?

No — and that's how we keep pricing affordable. Routine maintenance and reasonable content updates are included according to your plan. Major redesigns, new sections or custom features are scoped separately.
